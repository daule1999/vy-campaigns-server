## Executive Summary

Both Interakt and AiSensy are excellent WhatsApp Business API providers with different strengths. For **45,000 messages/month**, here's the quick verdict:

| Criteria | Winner | Reason |
|----------|--------|--------|

- Need multi-step customer journeys

- Abandoned cart recovery is important

#### 4. You Need Instagram + WhatsApp
- Manage both platforms from one inbox

- Cross-platform campaigns

#### 5. You Value Better Support
- Need reliable, fast customer service
- Have complex use cases
- Want dedicated account management

#### 1. Budget is Primary Concern
- Lower platform fees matter
- No markup on messages
- Don't need chatbot (or can add later)
- Simple use case

#### 2. You Do High-Volume D2C
- Need fast broadcast speeds
- 10k+ messages in minutes matters
- Time-sensitive flash sales

#### 3. Your Messages are Mostly Utility
- Order confirmations dominate
- Shipping updates are primary use

- Don't have existing CRM
