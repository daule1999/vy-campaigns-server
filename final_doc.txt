Question: "If we start using WhatsApp Business API, will WhatsApp Business App also work?"

Answer:

✅ YES, both can work together (Hybrid/Coexistence mode)
This is Meta's recommended approach for most businesses
Same number, messages sync automatically
You get flexibility of phone app + power of API
Only restriction: One must be disabled if you want >20 msg/sec throughput

How Hybrid Mode Works in Practice

Scenario 1: Agent on the Go
Agent's phone (WhatsApp Business App)
    ↕️ (syncs automatically)
BSP Platform (Aisensy/Interakt dashboard on computer)
Agent can reply from phone OR computer - both work!

Scenario 2: Automation + Manual
9 AM: API sends automated "Good morning" broadcast to 1000 customers
    ↓
Customer replies: "I need help in order #123"
    ↓
Agent sees message on WhatsApp Business App
    ↓
Agent replies personally from phone
    ↓
Conversation continues smoothly


Limitations of Hybrid Mode
Throughput: Limited to 20 messages/second (vs 500/sec on API-only)
Features: Some API features don't work (groups, disappearing messages)
App Required: WhatsApp Business App must stay installed
Region: Not available in EU, UK, Australia, Japan (as of 2025)

❌ API-ONLY MODE - Only One Works
If you choose API-only (traditional approach):

✅ Higher throughput (up to 500 messages/second)
❌ Cannot use WhatsApp Business App anymore
❌ Must delete your WhatsApp Business App account first
❌ NO chat history transfers

Feature	                Hybrid Mode (Both)	API-Only
WhatsApp Business App	✅ Works	            ❌ Must delete
WhatsApp Business API	✅ Works	            ✅ Works
Chat History Sync	    ✅ Up to 6 months	❌ None
Message Throughput	    20 msg/sec	         500 msg/sec
Use Phone for Chats	    ✅ Yes	            ❌ No
Automation	            ✅ Yes	            ✅ Yes


Question: "Will it sync our entire data (more than one year) before we start the API?"

Answer:

NO - Only last 6 months syncs
❌ Data older than 6 months will NOT sync
✅ You must manually export important old chats
✅ Old data stays accessible in WhatsApp Business App
✅ Going forward, everything syncs automatically
