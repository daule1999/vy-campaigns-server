## 💡 **Smart Cost-Saving Strategies**

### **Strategy 1: Maximize Service Messages (FREE)**
Encourage customers to message you first:
- Add WhatsApp QR codes in stores/website
- Use "Click to WhatsApp" buttons
- Promote WhatsApp as primary contact
- **Result**: Up to 50% of messages could be FREE

### **Strategy 2: Use Utility Instead of Marketing**
Convert promotional content to utility templates:
- Order updates with product suggestions
- Shipping notifications with upsells
- **Savings**: ₹0.74-0.94 per message

### **Strategy 3: Reply Within 24 Hours**
Set up alerts for customer messages:
- Respond within 24-hour window = FREE
- Automated "We received your message" reply
- **Result**: No charges for customer service

## 🧮 **Quick Cost Calculator**

Want to calculate for your exact mix? Use this formula:

```
Total Cost = Subscription + (Marketing_Count × Marketing_Rate) + (Utility_Count × Utility_Rate)

Example (Interakt):
= ₹2,299 + (8,000 × ₹0.882) + (12,000 × ₹0.16)
= ₹2,299 + ₹7,056 + ₹1,920
= ₹11,275/month
```
